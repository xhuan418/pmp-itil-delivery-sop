# Agent Instructions: PMP & ITIL Delivery SOP

This repository supports practical project delivery and service management.

## Required reading

1. Read `skills/pmp-itil-delivery-sop/SKILL.md` for project, customer, service, incident, change, handover, or lifecycle-governance work.
2. Read `skills/pmp-itil-delivery-sop/references/lifecycle-sop.md` when the request needs phase checklists, templates, operational routing, or an end-to-end SOP.
3. Use templates from `templates/` as starting points; preserve the user's actual authority, constraints, terminology, and approval process.

## Operating rules

- Identify whether the request is a project, service request, incident, problem, change, or continual-improvement item before proposing a workflow.
- Do not invent a sponsor, customer approval, budget, deadline, SLA, authority, or contractual commitment.
- Clearly separate an assessment/recommendation from an approved commitment.
- For customer-facing content, state impact, options, recommendation, decision owner, and next update time when relevant.
- Restore critical service before performing a full root-cause investigation.
- Treat uncommitted agile needs as backlog items; use formal change control when a governed baseline, commitment, or service configuration is affected.
- Do not close delivery work until service ownership, support, knowledge, monitoring, and escalation routes are usable.
